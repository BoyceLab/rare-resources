# Review methods and coverage

Review date: October 19, 2026.

## Purpose

This directory helps families, disabled people, patient organizations and research teams discover relevant services and information. It combines a broad directory with a focused review of transition, siblings, parenting, nonprofit capacity and registry infrastructure. It is not a systematic review, a complete directory of every condition, or an independent assessment of vendors.

## Search approach

Searches used official program, provider, government, academic, nonprofit and publisher pages. Discovery included related programs and directories linked from those pages, direct searches for service names, and targeted combinations of:

| Area | Search concepts |
| --- | --- |
| Siblings | Sibshops, sibling groups, adult siblings, young siblings, family weekends, sibling bereavement |
| Families | Parent-to-parent support, family-to-family centers, complex care, respite, early intervention, feeding, deafblind family support |
| Transition | Got Transition, adult healthcare, six core elements, youth readiness, postsecondary programs, employment, benefits, supported decision-making, housing |
| Nonprofit capacity | Patient organization training, governance, fundraising, research readiness, consent, patient engagement, compensation, grant programs |
| Registry options | Registry hosting, natural history, ePRO, eConsent, EDC, medical records, EHR integration, recruitment, data governance, standards |
| Practical assistance | Medication assistance, copay funds, case management, accessible camps, sibling and family programs |

Named tools and organizations were checked on their official sites where accessible. Existing research readings were retained and registry/partnership/transition guidance was added. Some readings were assessed through abstracts or available text rather than complete paywalled articles. No formal risk-of-bias assessment, database-wide search, dual screening or PRISMA process was performed.

## What the fields mean

| Field | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Content retrieved | A page or document was retrieved. This is not proof of an active service. |
| Indexed content found | Relevant official content was visible in search results; direct access was limited or incomplete. |
| Access restricted | Automated retrieval encountered an access restriction or challenge. |
| Unverified | The review could not confirm useful content at the destination. |
| Program status | Specific evidence about activity, intake, information availability or uncertainty. |
| Activity group | A broad filter derived from the program-status note. Read the full note before relying on it. |
| Review level | Whether only link access, the description, or description and status received review. |
| Checked | Date of review, not publication date or a promise of continuing availability. |

Resources with basic descriptions remain visible and clearly marked for further review. This preserves discovery breadth without implying that every program has current activity evidence.

## Activity and eligibility

Current official event dates, intake information and explicit announcements are stronger evidence of activity than a copyright year or an old grant acknowledgment. Older planning materials can still be useful, but their presence does not establish current staffing, funding or enrollment. A closed grant cycle is not the same as a closed organization.

Got Transition's official homepage reports an October 7, 2026 webinar and current transition guidance. The directory retains its tools with dates and activity evidence separated. Family programs retain local eligibility and availability notes. Assistance funds and camps can change availability quickly.

Registry descriptions distinguish nonprofit hosting programs, vendors, EDC, EHR/data connection, recruitment, infrastructure, standards and guidance. Retired offerings are excluded from current comparison options. Product descriptions were not tested through purchases, participant enrollment, data uploads or contract review.

## Data and inclusion limits

The directory has a strong United States and English-language emphasis, with selected international resources. Local service coverage, language access, cultural relevance and condition coverage are uneven. Parent and sibling support is not interchangeable with clinical mental-health treatment. Research recruitment is not treatment access. Information about benefits, rights or healthcare does not determine an individual's eligibility.

URL duplicates are merged after normalizing common hostname variants. Different tools from one organization remain separate when they serve distinct purposes. Category counts overlap and must not be added to obtain a total. Counts refer to resource entries, not unique organizations.

## Website validation

The release checks JSON structure, counts, IDs, internal file references, JavaScript syntax and key filter/export/print behavior. No live browser rendering or screen-reader audit was available in this environment; review the published site on your target devices and with assistive technology before treating accessibility as independently verified.

## Updating

Prefer a direct official link, a concise original description and dated evidence. Update a moved service to its current provider. Record access restrictions as uncertainty, not closure. Recheck time-sensitive grants, events, intake and benefit rules before sharing a specific recommendation.

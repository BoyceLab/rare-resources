Choosing a registry or data partner

Compare the role first: a registry host, study database, records connector and recruitment service solve different problems. Features below are provider descriptions, not tested capabilities or endorsements.

Ask about data ownership and export, consent and withdrawal, participant access, accessibility, languages, hosting locations, governance, ethics review, support, full costs and what happens if the service closes.
